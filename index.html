HOW TO PLAY

  • Swipe or tap two neighbours to swap them. Line up 3 or more of a kind.
  • 4 in a row makes a Nova: it blasts everything around it.
  • L or T shape makes a Cross: it clears its whole row and column.
  • 5 in a row makes a Prism: swap it with any piece to clear that colour.
  • Swap two specials together for mega combos. Cascades multiply your points!
  • Zen mode never ends: the board always has a move, and your game is saved.
